Aquasantec Celebrates Dual Recognition in Sustainability & Quality Excellence

Aquasantec is proud to announce two major achievements that reflect our commitment to sustainability, innovation, and trusted quality across Africa.

We placed Top 3 in Africa in the “Beyond Sustainability” category at the Sustainability & ESG Africa Awards 2025 in Johannesburg — recognizing our leadership in circular manufacturing, community empowerment, and responsible production.

We are also honored to receive the Quality Seal of Excellence at the East Africa Brand Quality Awards — a testament to our world-class standards, reliability, and continuous innovation.

These recognitions celebrate the dedication of our people, the trust of our customers, and our mission to build a more inclusive and sustainable Africa.

Excellence isn’t our goal — it’s our standard.
