Another Milestone – We Officially Join United Nations Global Impact

KENTAINERS LTD, an Aquasantec International Limited Group company, is proud to officially become a signatory to the UN Women’s Empowerment Principles (WEPs) — a global commitment to advancing gender equality under the world’s largest corporate sustainability initiative.

This marks a significant milestone in our journey toward embedding ethical, transparent, and sustainable practices across all areas of our operations.

As a member, we are committed to upholding the UNGC’s Ten Principles in the areas of:

  • ✔ Human Rights
  • ✔ Labour
  • ✔ Environment
  • ✔ Anti-Corruption

Joining the UNGC reinforces our commitment to corporate responsibility, aligning our long-term strategies with globally recognized sustainability standards.

At Kentainers, we believe building a better future starts with responsible action today.
