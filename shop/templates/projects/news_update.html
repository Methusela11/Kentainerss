News Update

PUBLIC CLARIFICATION

Following media reports regarding Kentainerss Limited and the explosion that occurred in Embakasi, Nairobi, we wish to provide clarification.

The explosion did not occur at our former premises in Embakasi either. We would like to confirm that Kentainerss Limited had moved its operations to Nairobi Gate Industrial Park, Northlands area.

We extend our sincere condolences to the families affected and thank the national police, Nairobi County Government, St. John’s Ambulance, and other first responders for their quick response.

Kentainerss Limited
2nd February 2024
