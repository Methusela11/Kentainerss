Supporting Recovery Through Water Security: Our Partnership with NACADA Kenya

Kentainers Ltd is proud to partner with NACADA Kenya in promoting access to clean and reliable water through the donation of a water tank to the Miritini Treatment & Rehabilitation Centre.

Water security is a critical pillar in creating safe, dignified spaces for recovery and wellness. We believe that sustainable access to water is not just a basic need — it’s a powerful tool in supporting health, hygiene, and long-term rehabilitation outcomes.

This initiative forms part of our ongoing Corporate Social Responsibility commitment to support communities through strategic partnerships.

Together, we are investing in the future of recovery and resilience across Kenya.
