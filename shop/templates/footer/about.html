About Kentainerss

Delivering durable water storage solutions for homes, industries, and communities across Kenya.

Who We Are

Kentainers Limited is one of Kenya's leading manufacturers of high-quality plastic water storage tanks and containers. Our mission is to provide reliable, durable, and sustainable water storage solutions for homes, businesses, institutions, and agricultural communities.

With decades of experience in manufacturing and innovation, we continue to invest in technology and quality processes that ensure our products meet international standards.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted provider of water storage solutions in Africa, empowering communities through reliable access to water.

Our Mission

To manufacture high-quality, durable, and innovative water storage products that meet the evolving needs of our customers.

Our Values

Integrity, innovation, sustainability, and customer satisfaction guide everything we do.

Our Manufacturing Facility

Our state-of-the-art production facility enables us to produce a wide range of water tanks and plastic products that meet international quality standards.
